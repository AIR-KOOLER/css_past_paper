Chemistry Physical Chemistry by Silbey, Alberty & Bawendi – Covers thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry and other physical chemistry principles in detail. Organic Chemistry by Morrison & Boyd – A classic text for understanding organic chemistry mechanisms and reactions, covering the organic portion of the syllabus. (Additionally, Concise Inorganic Chemistry by J.D. Lee is often recommended for inorganic chemistry topics, and solving past papers is crucial for this subject.)
