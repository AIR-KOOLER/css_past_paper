Balochi–English Dictionary by Dr. Naseer Dashti – Useful for building vocabulary and understanding classical Balochi texts, since translation is a part of the exam. Tareekh-e-Balochi Adab (History of Balochi Literature) by Zahoor Shah Hashmi – Covers the evolution of Balochi literature, major poets, and literary works; helpful for attempting literary questions in Balochi. (Familiarize yourself with prominent Balochi poets like Gul Khan Nasir and Atta Shad, and read some of their poetry. If possible, consult an academy guide for Balochi that covers expected questions and translations.)
