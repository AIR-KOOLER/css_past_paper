Philosophy Philosophy Made Simple by Richard H. Popkin & Avrum Stroll – An introductory text that explains key philosophical ideas and arguments in simple language, covering both Western and some Eastern philosophy basics. A History of Muslim Philosophy (Vol. I & II, ed. M.M. Sharif) – A collection of scholarly essays on the development of philosophical thought in the Muslim world, including figures like Al-Farabi, Al- Ghazali, Ibn Sina, and others. (For Western philosophy, Bertrand Russell’s History of Western Philosophy can be a useful overview. Also, Islamic Philosophy by M. Saeed Sheikh gives a concise picture of major Muslim philosophers . Utilizing the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy for specific topics is highly recommended for quick reference and clarification .)
