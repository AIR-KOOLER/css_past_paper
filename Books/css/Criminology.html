Criminology Criminology: Theories, Patterns, and Typologies by Larry J. Siegel – Covers classical and modern theories of crime, types of crimes, and the criminal justice system; a good introductory text for the subject. Understanding Criminology by Sandra Walklate – Focuses on contemporary criminological perspectives and debates, helping to address questions on crime and society with up-to-date examples. (It may also help to review Pakistan-specific crime data and laws (e.g., juvenile justice laws) and consult books like New Perspectives in Criminology by J.E. Conklin for additional theory .)
