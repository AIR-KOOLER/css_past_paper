Arabic A New Arabic Grammar of the Written Language by J.A. Haywood & H.M. Nahmad – A comprehensive grammar reference that covers classical Arabic syntax and morphology, useful for mastering translation and grammar questions. Tareekh Adab-e-Arabi (History of Arabic Literature) by Ahmad Hasan Zayyat (Urdu) – A history of Arabic literature outlining major periods (Jahiliyyah, early Islamic, Umayyad, Abbasid, Andalusian, etc.) and important literary figures/works in each era. (To prepare well, practice translating unseen Arabic passages and poetry. Study the prescribed texts (poems and prose) given in the FPSC syllabus. Using an Arabic–English dictionary like Hans Wehr for modern vocabulary and a classical Arabic glossary for older texts is highly recommended.)
