Islamic History & Culture The Spirit of Islam by Syed Ameer Ali – A classic work that provides insight into Islamic history, cultural heritage, and the contributions of Muslims to civilization. History of the Arabs by Philip K. Hitti – A comprehensive history of the Arab people, covering the pre-Islamic era through the rise of Islam and the Caliphates, which gives background for Islamic culture and civilization. (Additionally, Islam and the World by Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi is recommended for understanding the impact of Islam on global civilization . Candidates often refer to university notes that cover the political, social, and cultural history of the Muslim world to complement these readings.)
