Psychology Understanding Psychology by Robert S. Feldman – An introductory psychology textbook covering all major topics (learning, personality, abnormal psychology, etc.) in an easy-to-grasp manner. Psychology (CSS Edition) by Tayyab Shahid (JWT) – A concise book that follows the FPSC syllabus closely, providing exam-oriented explanations of psychological concepts and Pakistani case examples. (Another good reference is Psychology: Themes and Variations by Wayne Weiten for a more detailed study. Also, practice past paper questions to see which areas (e.g., developmental psychology, psychological testing) need more focus.)
