Constitutional Law Comparative Constitutional Law by Hamid Khan & M.W. Rana – Covers constitutional principles with comparisons between various countries’ constitutions, and includes Pakistan’s constitutional framework. Constitutional and Political History of Pakistan by Hamid Khan – (Also listed under Pakistan Affairs) Provides insight into the making and development of Pakistan’s constitutions (1956, 1962, 1973) and major constitutional cases . (Additionally, reading actual texts of world constitutions (UK, USA, France, etc.) and Selected Constitutions of the World by Dr. Sultan Khan can be helpful for Paper II of Constitutional Law.)
