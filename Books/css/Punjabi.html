Punjabi Dugngian Sochaan by Akhtar Husain Jafferi – A book on Punjabi literature and poetry (in Punjabi language), often recommended to cover the classical and modern Punjabi literary themes. Punjab Rang by Qamar Husnain Qadri & Faisal Hayat Jappa – Provides an overview of Punjabi literature, including prominent writers and poets, and covers the Punjabi language portion of the syllabus. (Candidates should be comfortable with writing Punjabi in Shahmukhi script and be familiar with famous works of Punjabi poetry (Bulleh Shah, Waris Shah, etc.) and prose.)
