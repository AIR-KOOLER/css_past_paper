Applied Mathematics An Introduction to Vector Analysis by Dr. Khalid Latif – Provides coverage of vector algebra and calculus, a key area in the Applied Mathematics syllabus. Introduction to Mechanics by Q.K. Ghori – Addresses dynamics and statics, covering classical mechanics problems relevant to the applied math portion. (Other useful texts include Khalid Latif’s An Intermediate Course in Theoretical Mechanics for advanced mechanics and Differential Equations with Boundary Value Problems by Zill & Cullen for the differential equations part of the syllabus.)
