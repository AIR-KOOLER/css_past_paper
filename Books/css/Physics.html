Physics Fundamentals of Physics by Halliday, Resnick & Krane – A foundational physics textbook covering mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, etc., suitable for conceptual clarity and problem practice. Concepts of Modern Physics by Arthur Beiser – Focuses on modern physics topics (quantum theory, atomic structure, nuclear physics) which are part of the higher-level physics syllabus. (For certain topics, refer to specialized sources: e.g. Electricity and Magnetism by Sadiku for electromagnetics , Thermodynamics by Eastop & McConkey for thermodynamics, and other standard university-level texts as needed.)
