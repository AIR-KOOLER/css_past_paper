Gender Studies 50 Key Concepts in Gender Studies by Jane Pilcher & Imelda Whelehan – An accessible guide explaining fundamental concepts and theories in the field of gender studies. Gender Studies by Amanullah Gondal – A comprehensive text covering the entire CSS Gender Studies syllabus, including feminist theories, gender issues in Pakistan, and global gender debates. (Additional reading: works by authors like Kamla Bhasin (Understanding Gender), Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie (We Should All Be Feminists), and Pakistani scholars like Rubina Saigol or Farzana Bari can provide valuable perspectives .)
