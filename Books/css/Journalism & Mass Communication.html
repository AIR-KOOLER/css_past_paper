Journalism & Mass Communication Mass Communication: Theory and Practice by Umair Khan (JWT) – A comprehensive book covering media theories, advertising, public relations, and the Pakistani media landscape, tailored for CSS candidates. Journalism for All by Dr. Mehdi Hasan – A fundamental book (available in both Urdu and English) that introduces the principles of journalism, news reporting and writing, and the role of media, with a focus on Pakistan’s media evolution. (In addition, Introduction to Mass Communication by Stanley J. Baran provides a global perspective on media literacy and the impact of mass media, and Dennis McQuail’s Mass Communication Theory is useful for deeper understanding of communication models and theories.)
