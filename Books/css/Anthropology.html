Anthropology Cultural Anthropology: The Human Challenge by William A. Haviland et al. – An accessible introduction to cultural anthropology, exploring concepts like culture, kinship, religion, and language with cross-cultural examples. Anthropology: Appreciating Human Diversity by Conrad Phillip Kottak – A comprehensive text that covers physical anthropology, archaeology, linguistics, and cultural anthropology, giving a broad overview of the field. (Additionally, Pakistani Society by Akbar S. Ahmed provides anthropological insights into Pakistani culture , and journals or articles on specific cultural practices can be useful for contemporary examples.)
