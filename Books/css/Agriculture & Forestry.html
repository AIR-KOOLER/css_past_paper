Agriculture & Forestry Agri-Forestry by Akhtar Abbas – A concise book focusing on agriculture and forestry topics, combining essential concepts of crop production, soil science, and forest management relevant to the CSS syllabus. Agriculture in Pakistan by Dr. Mohammad Hussain Khan – Discusses Pakistan’s agricultural sector, policies, and issues in detail, providing local context and statistics which are useful for paper examples. (Regularly reviewing the Economic Survey of Pakistan’s agriculture chapter and forestry statistics can also be beneficial .)
