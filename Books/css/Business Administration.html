Business Administration Marketing Management by Philip Kotler & Kevin Keller – Covers fundamental marketing concepts, strategies, and case studies; useful for the marketing section. Management by Stephen P. Robbins & Mary Coulter – A comprehensive management textbook covering organizational management theories and practices (planning, organizing, leading, controlling). Human Resource Management by Gary Dessler (with South Asian edition by Biju Varkkey) – Recommended for HR management topics like recruitment, training, performance appraisal, etc., included in the syllabus. (Also consult Principles of Managerial Finance by Gitman for the finance section , Operations Management by Jay Heizer & Barry Render for production/operations, and Supply Chain Management by Chopra & Meindl for supply chain topics. Some local publications like a compiled book by Syed Ahsan Zohaib cover all sections in one volume .)
