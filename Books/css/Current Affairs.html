Current Affairs Pakistan’s Foreign Policy 1947–2016: A Concise History by Abdul Sattar – An insightful overview of Pakistan’s foreign relations and policy, helpful for understanding current affairs in historical context. Pakistan: A Hard Country by Anatol Lieven – Provides deep analysis of Pakistan’s political and social structures, offering context for current issues. (In addition to books, regularly reading quality newspapers like Dawn, and magazines such as The Economist, Foreign Affairs, etc., is highly recommended for Current Affairs preparation.)
