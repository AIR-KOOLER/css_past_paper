Geology Physical Geology by Charles C. Plummer, David McGeary & Diane Carlson – An introductory text covering Earth’s structure, minerals, rocks, geological processes, and landscape development. Principles of Physical Geology by Arthur Holmes – A classic book providing a strong foundation in physical geology, including plate tectonics, earth materials, and historical geology. (Other references: Environmental Geology by C.W. Montgomery and regional geology resources like Geology of the Himalaya, Karakoram, Hindukush in Pakistan can be useful for specific topics .)
