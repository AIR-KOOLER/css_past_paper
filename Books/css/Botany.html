Botany Esau’s Plant Anatomy by Ray F. Evert & Susan E. Eichhorn – Covers plant structure and function (cells, tissues, and organs of plants) which helps in understanding foundational botanical concepts. Cryptogamic Botany (Volumes I & II) by G.M. Smith – These volumes (Algae & Fungi; Bryophytes & Pteridophytes) cover non-flowering plants and are useful for topics on lower plant forms and their life cycles. (For plant physiology and ecology sections, texts like Plant Physiology by Taiz & Zeiger and Fundamentals of Ecology by Eugene Odum are also recommended.)
