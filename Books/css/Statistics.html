Statistics Introduction to Statistical Theory by Sher Muhammad Chaudhry and Shahid Kamal – This two-part book (Part I & II) is a staple for covering probability, distributions, and statistical inference as per CSS syllabus (commonly used in M.A./M.Sc. statistics as well). Probability and Statistics for Engineers and Scientists by Ronald E. Walpole et al. – A helpful reference for probability theory, descriptive statistics, and inferential techniques, explained in an intuitive way. (It’s also useful to cover topics from Principles and Procedures of Statistics by Steel & Torrie for experimental statistics and to practice exercises from past CSS papers and FPSC-recommended problem sets .)
