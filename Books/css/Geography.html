Geography Human Geography: People, Place, and Culture by H.J. de Blij and Alexander B. Murphy – Covers human geography topics such as demographics, cultural patterns, urbanization, and economic activities with case studies. Physical Geography by H.J. de Blij & Peter O. Muller – Focuses on physical geography including climatology, geomorphology, hydrology, biogeography, and maps, which aligns with the physical geography part of the syllabus. (A local publication like Comprehensive Geography by Jahangir Sanpal is also handy for covering regional geography of Pakistan. Candidates should use atlases and practice map-drawing for geographical distributions.)
