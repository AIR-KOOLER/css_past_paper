British History History of England by P.C. Thomas – A two-part book covering British history from ancient times up to the modern period; useful for the Paper I (earlier periods). English History 1914–1945 by A.J.P. Taylor – Focuses on early 20th-century British history (from World War I through World War II), covering the political, social, and economic developments of that era. (For broader coverage, Mastering Modern British History by Norman Lowe is an excellent resource that spans 19th and 20th century British history , and Kenneth O. Morgan’s works (e.g., The People’s Peace: British History 1945-1990) cover post-WWII developments .)
