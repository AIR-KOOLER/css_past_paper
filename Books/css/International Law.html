International Law International Law by Malcolm N. Shaw – A comprehensive and authoritative text on public international law covering sources, statehood, diplomacy, use of force, human rights, etc. International Law by J.G. Starke – A classic book that provides clear explanations of international law principles, often recommended for beginners to grasp fundamentals. (For case studies and advanced topics, Cases and Materials on International Law by D.J. Harris is useful . Also, Principles of Public International Law by Ian Brownlie is another authoritative source.)
