2006-2010

Underlying Patterns and Repeating Questions in CSS Journalism Exams (2006–2010)

1. Mass Communication

  1. Pattern: Definitions, process, importance, barriers, and relevance in the modern age.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2006 Q1: "What do you know about Mass Communication? Describe its importance in the present age."
  4. 2008 Q2: "Discuss the concept and process of communication... importance of Mass Communication."
  5. 2010 Q2: "Mass Communication is considered important in modern age. Discuss the concept and process."

2. News

  1. Pattern: Functions, values, components, societal impact, and differentiation from related concepts.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2006 Q2: "Explain what is news, its functions and values."
  4. 2008 Q3: "‘News’ is the real soul of Journalism... major functions and values."
  5. 2010 Q4: "Describe news ‘which is the real soul of journalism’ with examples."

3. Role of Media in Pakistan

  1. Pattern: Focus on historical and contemporary roles of print/electronic media, challenges, and social impact.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2007 Q2: "Give your evaluation of the role of print journalism in Pakistan."
  4. 2008 Q4: "Critically evaluate the role of present TV Channels in social development."
  5. 2009 Q6: "How ‘Print Media’ is important after the development of ‘Electronic Media’?"

4. Historical Aspects (Muslim Press & Pakistan Movement)

  1. Pattern: Role of Muslim Press during the Pakistan Movement, key figures, and challenges.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2006 Q4: "Explain the difficulties faced by the Muslim Press during the movement of Pakistan."
  4. 2008 Q5: "Discuss the role played by the Muslim Press during Pakistan Movement."
  5. 2010 Q5: "Critically examine the role of Muslim press during the Pakistan Movement."

5. Public Relations & Advertising

  1. Pattern: Definitions, roles, principles, trends, and organizational impact.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2006 Q5/Q6: "Public Relations is actually Image Building activity."
  4. 2008 Q7: "Define ‘Advertising’... principles of successful advertising."
  5. 2009 Q8: "Difference between PRO and Personal Assistant... responsibilities of a good PRO."

6. Journalism Terms (MCQs)

  1. Pattern: Repeated testing of basic journalism terminology, historical firsts, and abbreviations.
  2. Repeating MCQ Topics:
  3. ABC: Audit Bureau of Circulation (2006, 2008, 2010).
  4. Byline: Reporter’s name with story (2006, 2008, 2010).
  5. Press Laws: 1963 Press Ordinance (2006, 2008, 2010).
  6. Yellow Journalism: Sensationalism (2006, 2009, 2010).

7. Press Freedom & Ethics

  1. Pattern: Debates on freedom vs. responsibility, press codes, and global comparisons.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2008 Q9: "‘Freedom of Press’ and ‘Responsibility’... evaluate the case of Pakistan."
  4. 2009 Q9: "Describe an ideal ‘Press Code of Ethics’ for Pakistani journalists."
  5. 2010 Q9: "Discuss ‘Free Press’ and ‘Responsibility’ in developing/developed societies."

8. Electronic Media Trends

  1. Pattern: Growth, challenges, and societal impact of TV channels.
  2. Repeating Questions:
  3. 2007 Q3: "Survey the development of electronic media in Pakistan."
  4. 2009 Q5: "Evaluate critically the performance of private TV channels in Pakistan."

Key Takeaways

  1. High-Frequency Topics: Mass Communication, News, Muslim Press, PR/Advertising, and Media Ethics.
  2. MCQ Focus: Definitions (e.g., embargo, byline), historical firsts (e.g., first Urdu newspaper), and laws (e.g., PEMRA).
  3. Consistent Structure: Compulsory MCQs + Essay sections split into theory and applied questions.

Years with Overlapping Content:

  1. 2006–2008: Heavy focus on foundational concepts.
  2. 2009–2010: Shift toward ethics, globalization, and modern media challenges.

Candidates should prioritize mastering recurring themes and terminology while practicing analytical writing on media roles and ethics.
