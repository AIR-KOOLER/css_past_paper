2004-2010

Underlying Patterns and Repeating Questions in Geology Exams (2004–2010)

1. Recurring Themes

  • Mineralogy & Petrology
    • Repeated Topics: Feldspar, Pyroxene, and Phyllosilicate groups; Bowen’s Reaction Series; igneous/metamorphic rock classification (e.g., syenite, eclogite, peridotite).
    • Years: 2004 (Q3, Q8), 2005 (Q1), 2009 (MCQs i, iii, vii), 2010 (MCQ xiv).
  • Structural Geology
    • Repeated Topics: Faults (Chaman Fault, Horst-Graben), folds (Chevron folds, Balochistan folds), rock failure theory.
    • Years: 2004 (Q5), 2005 (Q2), 2009 (Q8, MCQs xiv–xvii), 2010 (Q2).
  • Stratigraphy & Paleontology
    • Repeated Topics: Tertiary sequence in Sindh, Salt Range Paleozoic/Mesozoic rocks, cephalopod/brachiopod morphology, index fossils.
    • Years: 2004 (Q4, Q7), 2005 (Q6), 2009 (Q6, Q8), 2010 (MCQ xix).
  • Plate Tectonics & Earthquakes
    • Repeated Topics: Plate boundaries (Indian Plate focus), earthquake epicenter location, seismic-resistant structures.
    • Years: 2004 (Q2, Q6), 2009 (Q2, MCQs xii–xiii), 2010 (Q8).
  • Applied Geology
    • Repeated Topics: Dam site selection (Mangla/Tarbela silting), groundwater (Darcy’s Law, aquifers), mineral/hydrocarbon exploration (Saindak, Sui gas field).
    • Years: 2004 (Q3–Q6), 2009 (Q3–Q6), 2010 (Q3, Q6–Q7).

2. Compulsory Short Notes

  • Consistent Terms:
    • Structural Terms: Turbidites, Asthenosphere, Cross bedding, Submarine fan, Marker bed.
    • Minerals/Rocks: Pyroxene, Kaolinite, Chert, Arkose, Garnet.
    • Processes: Enfoliation, Birefringence, Lithification, Fossilization.
    • Regional Features: Chaman Fault, Main Mantle Thrust, Jhlum Group.
    • Years: 2004 (Q8), 2005 (Q8), 2009 (Q8), 2010 (Q8).

3. Regional Focus on Pakistan

  • Key Areas:
    • Indus Basin: Reservoir rocks, hydrocarbon fields (Kohat-Potwar Basin).
    • Balochistan: Volcanic activity, fold types.
    • Sindh: Tertiary strata, Thar coal.
    • Salt Range: Paleozoic/Mesozoic stratigraphy.
    • Years: 2004 (Q4, Q5), 2009 (Q2, Q6), 2010 (MCQ xx).

4. Repeated Question Types

  • MCQs (2009–2010):
    • Bowen’s Reaction Series (2009 vi).
    • Metamorphic rock progression (2009 x).
    • Aquifer types (2010 viii–ix).
    • Mineral hardness/chemistry (2009 i–iii).
  • Long Essays:
    • Magma & Igneous Rocks: Origin of magma, differentiation (2004 Q2, 2009 Q3).
    • Coal & Energy: Thar coal problems, future prospects (2004 Q2, 2009 Q7).
    • Earthquake Measures: Seismic zones in Pakistan (2004 Q6, 2009 MCQs xii).

5. Evolution of Exam Format

  • 2004–2005: Essay-only format with compulsory short notes.
  • 2009–2010: Introduction of MCQs (Part I) + essays (Part II).
  • Shift in Focus: Testing factual recall (MCQs) alongside applied knowledge (essays).

High-Yield Topics for Preparation

  1. Mineralogy: Feldspar, pyroxene, and silicate structures.
  2. Structural Geology: Faults (Chaman), folds, rock failure.
  3. Stratigraphy: Salt Range, Sindh Tertiary sequence.
  4. Applied Geology: Dams (Tarbela/Mangla), groundwater (Darcy’s Law), hydrocarbon exploration.
  5. Compulsory Terms: Memorize definitions for recurring short-note terms (e.g., Turbidites, Asthenosphere).

  • Repeating Questions:

    • 2017 Q2: Nesosilicates (structure + olivine properties).
    • 2019 Q3: Plagioclase series (physical/optical properties).
    • 2020 Q4: Silicate classification + isomorphism/polymorphism.
  • Pattern:
    • Emphasis on silicate subclasses (neso-, plagioclase) and economic mineral uses.

  • Repeating Questions:
    • 2016 Q4: Himalayas’ tectonic framework (P).
    • 2016 Q5: Metamorphic facies and plate boundaries.
    • 2017 Q3: Metamorphic facies during subduction.
    • 2018 Q5b: Plate boundaries and associated features.
    • 2019 Q4: Pull-apart basins (P).
    • 2020 Q5: Lineation vs. foliation.
  • Pattern:
    • Frequent focus on plate boundaries (convergent, transform), regional tectonics (Himalayas, Pakistan), and structural features.

4. Stratigraphy and Sedimentology

  • Repeating Questions:
    • 2017 Q4: Fossilization processes.
    • 2018 Q3b: Lithostratigraphic units.
    • 2019 Q2: Formation designation + Salt Range (P).
    • 2020 Q3: Stratigraphic laws + units.
  • Pattern:
    • Key topics: lithostratigraphy, fossil interpretation, and Pakistan’s stratigraphy (e.g., Salt Range).

  • Repeating Questions:
    • 2016 Q3/Q6: Mineral classes + electromagnetic exploration.
    • 2017 Q6: Oil/gas prospecting methods.
    • 2018 Q7: Hydrocarbon traps.
    • 2019 Q5/Q6: Pegmatites + ore exploration.
    • 2020 Q7: Hydrocarbon source vs. reservoir rocks.
  • Pattern:
    • Heavy emphasis on mineral/hydrocarbon exploration, classification, and Pakistan’s resources (e.g., 2018 Q8b, 2020 Q8c).

6. Geophysics and Exploration Methods

  • Repeating Questions:
    • 2016 Q6: Electromagnetic methods.
    • 2018 Q6: Gravity anomalies.
    • 2019 Q6: Geophysical methods for ores.
  • Pattern:
    • Focus on geophysical techniques (gravity, EM) and data interpretation.

  • Repeating Questions:
    • 2016 Q7: Earthquakes/tsunamis.
    • 2017 Q7: Landslides (P).
    • 2019 Q7: Volcanic hazards.
  • Pattern:
    • Hazards linked to tectonics (earthquakes), slope stability (landslides), and volcanic activity.

8. Short Notes

  • Recurring Topics (2016–2020):
    • Polarizing microscope (2017).
    • Coal (2020), Copper deposits (2018), Radioactive deposits (2020) (P).
    • Hydrocarbon sources (2019), Geothermal energy (2017).
    • Dam/tunnel site investigations (2019, 2020).

Key Takeaways

  1. Pakistan-Specific Geology: Salt Range, Indian Plate tectonics, landslides, and mineral deposits are recurring themes.
  2. High-Frequency Topics:
    1. Plate tectonics (every year).
    2. Mineralogy (2016–2020).
    3. Hydrocarbon systems (2016–2020).
  3. Diagrams/Sketches: Often required (e.g., 2017 Q3, 2018 Q5).
  4. Stratigraphic Laws: Steno’s principles, superposition (2020 Q3).

Advice for Preparation: Prioritize plate tectonics, mineral classification, Pakistan’s geology, and economic resources. Practice diagram-based answers for metamorphic facies, structural features, and stratigraphy.
